About M Square Vellore

Your home in Vellore, for a night or a season.

Quiet, well-run serviced apartments close to the CMC campus, made for people who stay a little longer.

Search stays Contact us

Hospitality that stays out of the way.

M Square was set up for the people who come to Vellore and stay a while.

Families here for treatment at CMC, professionals on assignment, travellers who want more than a hotel room: they all need the same thing. A calm place to rest, and a team that quietly takes care of the rest.

So we pair the space of an apartment with the support of a hotel. You get your own kitchen and living area, along with housekeeping, a front desk and secure access whenever you need them.

How we run the place.

Quiet by design

Calm rooms and a calm building, so you can rest properly after a long day.

Clear from the start

Rates, inclusions and policies are shown before you book, and every charge on your bill can be traced.

Made for longer stays

An equipped kitchen, laundry and room to spread out, because a week feels different from a night.

Someone always there

Our front desk is open around the clock, whether you arrive at midnight or need help at dawn.

Who we host.

Every guest comes to Vellore for a different reason. The stay is built around it.

Medical visits

Close to the CMC campus, with a quiet place for patients and families to rest between appointments.

Business travel

Reliable Wi-Fi, a room you can work in, and easy access to the city's main business district.

Extended stays

Weekly and monthly stays with housekeeping, laundry and a kitchen that makes it feel like home.

Family and leisure

Space for everyone, and a comfortable base for visiting Vellore Fort and the Sripuram Golden Temple.

Find us in Vellore.

Our flagship property sits in the heart of the city, near the CMC campus and the main business district.
